Hi, I'm Andrea. I build small, playful web apps with AI tools.
I studied IT, ended up in marketing, and kept one foot in design. Building is where the three meet.

**Things I've made**
- [Pinky Promise](https://pinkypromise.ink) – make a promise with up to 5 friends, sign it by hand, get reminded to keep it
- [Poetic Dictionary](https://poeticdictionary.ink) – a growing collection of untranslatable words
- [Moon Journal](https://hanacoder.github.io/moon-journal/) – shows which sign the moon is in tonight, so you have something to write about
- Little Hill Cinema – a private app for my friends' movie club: suggest a film, reserve a seat, spin to pick (code's private, the story's on Substack)

I write about how I build on [Substack](https://substack.com/@aihana) and about brands on [Medium](https://medium.com/@ai_hana). Based in Podgorica, Montenegro.
